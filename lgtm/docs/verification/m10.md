## How this module was verified

- The owner allowed the namespace `learn-m10` and the practice tenant `learn-m10` in this session and signed in to Grafana in the browser pane. The lab seed was given but no step uses a token.
- Every practice configuration (steps 5, 6, 7 and 9) was applied in `learn-m10` and its result read back from Loki, Mimir and Tempo through `http://loki|mimir|tempo.lab.k8s.local` with `X-Scope-OrgID: learn-m10`, between 12:27 and 12:45 UTC.
- Step 8 ran `alloy convert` and `alloy validate` from the image the lab pins, `docker.io/grafana/alloy:v1.21.3`, in a short-lived pod in `learn-m10` (`kubectl run --rm -i`). Docker Desktop was not running on the PC, and the session does not start the owner's applications.
- The lab's own Alloys were only read: `kubectl get`, a port-forward to `daemonset/k8s-monitoring-alloy-logs` on 127.0.0.1:18100, and the screen of `alloy-singleton` at `http://alloy.lab.k8s.local`. Reading the Secret `loki-k8s-monitoring` was refused by the session's guard and not needed: the tenant is in the values file and in the UI.
- From 12:49 UTC every practice step was run a second time from the step files as the site renders them (the commands and files taken from the MDX). Steps 5 and 6 passed. At 12:50 UTC new pods on worker2, worker3 and worker4 stopped starting (`plugin type="calico" failed (add): error getting ClusterInformation: connection is unauthorized: Unauthorized`), the Calico token fault on the open items list. For the rest of the rerun the practice Alloy, the telemetrygen Job and the convert pods were pinned to worker1 (`nodeSelector`, `--overrides` with `nodeName`); the pin is in no lesson file. Steps 7, 8 and 9 then passed between 13:08 and 13:13 UTC, and `learn-m10` was deleted at 13:13 UTC (`namespaces "learn-m10" not found`).
- After the planner restarted calico-node on worker2 to worker4 (13:13 to 13:15 UTC), steps 5 to 9 were run a third time from the step files with no pin, between 13:16:54 and 13:21:08 UTC: the practice Alloy ran on worker2 and worker1, telemetrygen and the convert pods were placed by the scheduler. Every action gave the result logged below (`loki.source.kubernetes.app`, `prometheus.scrape.app`, `rootTraceName` `lets-go`, the converter's critical error, report and two validation errors then `valid`, the `level` stream without `pod`, `reason="probe"`), and `learn-m10` was deleted again (`namespaces "learn-m10" not found` at 13:21 UTC).
