Scope: the whole SAA-C03 plan app (site/index.html, style.css, fx.css, ground.js, fx.js): overview, day flow, dialogs, celebrations. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the owner, one learner, works one study step at a time and checks where the plan stands; daily, on a wide desktop first, also phone and tablet.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The tracker as the owner's Excimer poster: a near-black sheet ruled by a yellow-tinted modular grid, where the day's work is the one subject and yellow marks lock onto it. Refuses the generic dark dashboard of equal cards and neon accents.

OWN-WORLD: Datum components (cut panels, frames, hairlines, mono labels, dot-matrix numerals) in Excimer colours: paper #111, charcoal plates, one cadmium yellow #F7D123 for the live thing, each view's one bold sentence-case Saira title and the crosshair/arrow marks; olive-yellow grid hairlines; days as element boxes; poster ground (grid with crossing dots, crosshairs, plates with halftones, black discs, a ring, yellow filaments) behind everything.

STORY: The owner opens the app, sees today's day and next step at once under the big yellow title, presses Start, works a card at a time, and is rewarded on a rising ladder of celebrations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Overview: rail on top (yellow code block with the poster mark), the yellow title "Solutions Architect Associate" large at left on a black plate, the plan's numbers as yellow key: value lines at right, header buttons under them; below, the hero frame (yellow dot-matrix day number, title, goal, numbers) beside the next-step panel with the one yellow Start button; crosshairs on the corners of that pair. Day view: yellow "Day N" title, step strip, the card with its sidebar, Back / Mark complete / Next fixed at the foot.

FORM: Pinned whole by the owner (Datum design system + the Excimer poster's colours and aesthetics). seed: none (pinned) — "use it to completely redesign the saa plan look and feel. With nice animations and effects. Also use the color scheme derived from the attached image (with aesthetics)". Signature interaction: the arrival, where the ground plots itself (grid draws, crosshairs lock on, filaments trace) and the subject's corner crosshairs lock on; a new step gets a yellow scan line. Motion grammar: eased cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) travel, linear switches, one live element per view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
